Simple terms for a simple tool.

Reading Comfort Pacer is free, open-source software that offers ergonomic prompts at boundaries you choose.

Not medical advice

The extension is a general comfort utility, not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, or substitute for professional care. Stop using it if it causes discomfort and seek qualified advice for persistent vision symptoms.

Your control

You decide when to begin or end a distance break and can disable reminders completely. Do not use the interface when doing so would be unsafe.
