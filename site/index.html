How it works

Choose when to pause.

When the timer ends, the extension waits for you to finish the paragraph or task.

  1. Read without interruption

    When the timer ends, it marks a reminder as ready.

  2. Finish your stopping point

    Use the extension icon to start the break when you are ready.

  3. Look beyond the display

    A full-screen timer runs for 20, 30, or 60 seconds.

Distance break

See the distance-break screen.

Look at a far object you can see. Pause the gentle motion if you want. Your device’s Reduce Motion setting turns it off.

Install the Chrome extension manually.

Download this build. The archive contains the folder you load in Chrome.

Download extension ↓
  1. Download and unzip the extension.
  2. Open chrome://extensions or your browser’s extension page.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin the extension icon, choose an interval, and keep reading.
