Privacy in plain words.

The extension keeps its reminder settings and aggregate break counts in your browser.

What the extension stores

It stores your interval, break length, vibration choice, enabled state, reminder time, and aggregate break counts. This data remains in local extension storage.

What it does not access

The extension has no camera, microphone, browsing-history, page-content, location, or identity permission. It has no account or telemetry feature.

Website data

This site does not use analytics or cookies. Delivery infrastructure may temporarily log an IP address and user agent for security.

Delete your data

Remove the extension to remove its local storage. You can also turn off reminders and keep your settings.
