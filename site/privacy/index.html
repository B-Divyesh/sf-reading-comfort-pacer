Privacy without fine print.

Reading Comfort Pacer works locally. We do not receive your reading activity, settings, or break history.

What the extension stores

The extension stores your selected reminder interval, break duration, vibration preference, enabled state, next reminder time, and aggregate counts of breaks accepted, snoozed, and completed. This information stays in your browser’s local extension storage.

What it never accesses

The extension does not request camera, microphone, browsing-history, page-content, location, identity, advertising, or health-data permissions. It has no account system and sends no telemetry.

Website data

This static website does not set cookies or run analytics. Standard infrastructure logs may temporarily contain an IP address and user agent for security and delivery reliability.

Delete your data

Remove the extension to delete its local storage. You can also disable reminders without deleting your preferences.

Contact

Questions can be filed in the project’s public issue tracker. This policy will be updated here if the product’s data practices change.
